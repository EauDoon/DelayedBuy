Sleep on it.

In the reservation rehearsal, a simulated merchant can accept a request for one fictional lamp at a fixed price for 24 hours. Nothing is due and no purchase takes place.

In the separate demo-funded checkout, creating a demo hold sets aside demo funds and fixes your price for the Arc table lamp. This demo reserves no stock.

The hold has no automatic expiry. After the wait, choose Complete purchase, then Confirm purchase to buy. You can cancel any time before buying.

Enable JavaScript to choose a waiting period and try checkout. No money moves.

In the separate report model, S$100 held for seven days at an assumed 4% annual rate earns about S$0.0767 gross. The shopper’s modeled cancellation share is S$0.06. The checkout simulation pays no interest.
